Questions

What appears on my page?

A small 6thSTAR, right under your review stars. A shopper points at it (or taps it on a phone) and one short line opens. Nothing else on your page changes.

Which themes work?

Themes with app blocks, such as Shopify's own Horizon: you place the 6thSTAR line under your review stars in the theme editor, and the guide shows each click. On a theme without app blocks there's nothing to place; your star still shows.

What data does it read?

Your products, photos and descriptions; your orders and returns, with return reasons and the notes buyers write; your return policy; your reviews; and your public website, to write your brief. It doesn't ask Shopify for a buyer's name, email, phone number or address. Privacy has the detail.

How long until my lines are ready?

Installing takes about 20 minutes. Writing your lines then depends on how many products you have. You read them before anything goes live.

What happens if I uninstall?

Uninstall from 6thSTAR's Settings and it clears everything it added: the line, the description sentences, the AI shoppers field and its records. If you uninstall from Shopify's own screen instead, the description sentences stay until you remove them.

What does the beta involve?

A small group of Shopify stores install 6thSTAR before it's listed. We set it up with you on a call, you read your lines before anything goes live, and you tell us what you see.
