Questions

Short answers to what retailers ask first.

What appears on my page?

A small 6thSTAR™, right under your review stars. A shopper points at it (or taps it on a phone) and the 6thSTAR™ phrase opens. Nothing else on your page changes.

Which themes work?

Themes with app blocks, such as Shopify's own Horizon: you place the 6thSTAR™ phrase under your review stars in the theme editor, and 6thSTAR™'s setup guide shows each click. On a theme without app blocks there's nothing to place; your star still shows.

What data does it read?

Your products, photos and descriptions; your orders and returns, with return reasons and the notes buyers write; your return policy; your reviews; and your public website, to write your brief. It doesn't ask Shopify for a buyer's name, email, phone number or address.

How long until my 6thSTAR™ phrases are ready?

Installing takes about 20 minutes. Writing your 6thSTAR™ phrases then depends on how many products you have. You read them before anything goes live.

Will it cost me sales?

No. Each 6thSTAR™ phrase first confirms the shopper's choice, then adds the one thing worth knowing, the way a friend would.

What happens if I uninstall?

Uninstall from 6thSTAR™'s Settings and it clears everything it added: the 6thSTAR™ phrase, the description sentences, the AI shoppers field and its records. If you uninstall from Shopify's own screen instead, the description sentences stay until you remove them.
