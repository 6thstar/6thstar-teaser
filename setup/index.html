Setup guides

Each step in order. Point at a step to see it large.Tap a step to see it large.

Place the 6thSTAR line under your review stars

  1. 1
    In Shopify, click Online Store.
  2. 2
    On your live theme, click Edit theme.
  3. 3
    At the top, open the page menu and choose Products › Default product.
  4. 4
    In the left panel, find Review stars.
  5. 5
    Point just below Review stars. Click the + that appears.
  6. 6
    Click Apps, then 6thSTAR line.
  7. 7
    Check that the 6thSTAR line sits right under your stars. Click Save.
  8. 8
    Done. Back in 6thSTAR, this step shows ✓ and Turn on is ready.

Don't see Review stars? Your reviews app may need to add them first. Write to trosen@rosengroup.us.

Theme without app blocks? There's nothing to do. Your star still shows.

Moved it later? 6thSTAR will tell you, and this guide will open again.

Share with All Shoppers

  1. Before you start
    Before you start: your store must be open to shoppers (no password) and qualify for Shopify Catalog. Check in Shopify
  2. 1
    In Shopify, click Agentic. Under Sources, click Shopify Catalog's status.
  3. 2
    Click Review your product data. This opens Channel mapping.
  4. 3
    Click into Product description, then ⊕, to see its sources. 6thSTAR agent description is under Product metafields.
  5. 4
    Take out Product description first, then add 6thSTAR agent description. Click Save.
  6. 5
    Done. Back in 6thSTAR, the AI shoppers card shows a green dot beside Through Shopify.

Shopify sends this to AI shoppers such as ChatGPT and Copilot. When each one reads it is up to them.

To undo it, set Product description back to your own description.
